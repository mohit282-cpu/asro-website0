ASRO What is IoT? A Guide for Students

Published by ASRO Content Team | 4 min read

The Internet of Things, or IoT, refers to the billions of physical devices around the world that are now connected to the internet, all collecting and sharing data.

Why IoT Matters in Nepal

From smart agriculture that optimizes water usage to automated weather stations, IoT has massive potential to solve local challenges in Nepal. ASRO's IoT training programs equip students with the skills to build these very systems.
